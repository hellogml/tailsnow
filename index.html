Frequently Asked Questions

Is the online coin flip really random?

Yes. Each flip uses your browser’s cryptographically secure random number generator, so heads and tails are equally likely on every toss.

Can I flip more than one coin at once?

Yes. Choose how many coins to flip and the tool returns each individual result together with the heads and tails tally.

Does it keep a history of flips?

Yes. Every result is added to the live tally so you can watch the running counts and check that the distribution stays fair.

Is the coin flip free?

It is free, works on any device, and needs no app or sign-up.
